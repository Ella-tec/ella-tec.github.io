A CLOSER LOOK · PRODUCT VISION

One connected workspace for finance, controls, and clarity.

FINOVA is being shaped around the day-to-day work of accounting teams: collecting evidence, reviewing balances, managing close activities, preparing reports, and keeping clients and reviewers connected.

01 / DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

Documents & Sample Library

Organize accounting and audit files, use a curated library of 40+ sample resources, and explore OCR / AI-assisted document analysis. A File Sanitizer is designed as a security checkpoint before documents reach AI processing.

DOCUMENT CONTROL · OCR / AI CONCEPT · PRE-PROCESSING SECURITY
02 / RECONCILIATION

Bank Reconciliation

Bring bank statement activity and accounting records into a review workflow, with AI-assisted matching intended to help identify likely matches and items that need human attention.

TRANSACTION REVIEW · MATCHING SUPPORT · EXCEPTIONS
03 / REPORTING & CLOSE

Financial Statements & Month-End

Connect trial-balance information to financial statement preparation and organize period-end work with a close checklist, so teams can see what is prepared, outstanding, and ready for review.

TRIAL BALANCE · STATEMENTS · CLOSE CHECKLIST
04 / AUDIT & CLIENT COLLABORATION

Audit, PBC & Client Portal

Structure audit requests, supporting documents, and workpaper activity while giving clients a dedicated portal experience for participating in the information-gathering process.

PBC REQUESTS · WORKPAPERS · CLIENT ACCESS
05 / MULTI-ENTITY

Entities & Consolidation

Support a multi-entity operating model, with a consolidation workspace intended to bring entity-level financial information together, including planned non-controlling-interest handling.

ENTITY MANAGEMENT · GROUP VIEW · NCI DIRECTION
06 / ASSISTANCE & ENABLEMENT

AI Copilot, Learning & Administration

Bring an AI assistant into the finance workspace alongside user invitations, role-oriented access, learning courses, progress tracking, and administration tools.

AI ASSISTANCE · USER MANAGEMENT · LEARNING
HOW THE WORK CONNECTS

From source document to reviewed financial information.

  1. CollectUpload records and organize evidence.
  2. PrepareCapture transactions and reconcile supporting data.
  3. ReviewInvestigate differences and complete close tasks.
  4. ReportPrepare statements and share approved information.
ENGINEERING FOUNDATION

Next.js 15 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Prisma ORM · SQLite demo database · NextAuth credentials authentication, with optional Microsoft Entra ID integration.

TRANSPARENT PROJECT STATUS

FINOVA is actively being developed. The operations workspace currently captures draft records and creates audit-log events. It does not yet perform full double-entry journal posting, production-grade PostgreSQL migration, payroll or tax calculations, or every planned approval and automation flow. AI/OCR and matching capabilities are described as product workflows, not guarantees of completed automation.
